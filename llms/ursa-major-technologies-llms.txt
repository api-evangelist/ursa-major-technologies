# Ursa Major Technologies

> Ursa Major Technologies Inc. is an American aerospace and defense propulsion manufacturer founded in 2015 by Joe Laurienti, a former SpaceX and Blue Origin propulsion engineer, headquartered in Berthoud, Colorado with additive manufacturing operations in Youngstown, Ohio. It designs, builds and hot-fire tests liquid rocket engines, solid rocket motors and in-space propulsion systems for space launch, hypersonics and U.S. and allied defense customers — including the 5,000 lbf Hadley oxygen-rich staged-combustion engine and the storable, throttleable, restartable Draper engine. Ursa Major was the first American company to fire an oxygen-rich staged combustion engine, states it has delivered more than 100 engines and logged over 100,000 seconds of hotfire testing, and builds engines and motors that are more than 80% 3D printed by mass. It is AS9100D, ISO 9001 and CMMC Level 2 certified.

## API surface

Ursa Major Technologies is an aerospace and defense hardware manufacturer selling to government and prime-contractor customers. As of 2026-08-05 it publishes **no** public API, developer portal, SDK, CLI, machine-readable API contract (OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC), MCP server or A2A agent card.

Candidate hosts `api.`, `developer.`, `developers.`, `docs.`, `portal.`, `status.`, `trust.`, `app.` and `mcp.ursamajor.com` have no DNS A record. The WordPress REST API at `/wp-json/` answers 403 to anonymous callers and is CMS boilerplate, not a product API. No first-party GitHub organization, npm package or PyPI package exists.
